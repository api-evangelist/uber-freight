The logistics industry is at a turning point. Advancements in artificial intelligence are reshaping how goods move across supply chains, making freight transportation more efficient, reliable, and scalable. At the forefront of this transformation is Physical AI—AI that enables machines to interact with and navigate the physical world, including self-driving trucks.
